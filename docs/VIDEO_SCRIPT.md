# Demo video script (2:55)

## Storyboard

Make the viewer feel the problem is theirs first, then show Neophi. **Part 1, without Neophi:** a person saves two Reels on Instagram (a DSA sheet, and a dance Reel for the red shirt), and a few days later cannot find either one or remember what mattered. **Part 2, with Neophi:** the same two saves are shared to Neophi with a few words; later, Journey shows a DSA branch with the key points right there, and the shirt save shows the person's own words "red shirt" first. Close: these were two examples; almost every save is a decision that gets lost; Neophi keeps it.

Narrator lines are in Hindi (Devanagari); the English line is for on-screen subtitles.

## Demo Reels: captions and notes

Use **sample Reels** you made yourselves (filmed, or generated with Gemini Veo 3.1). Do not use other creators' Reels, faces or music. Put *Sample Reels* in the video description.

**DSA Reel caption** (a made-up sheet, not any real creator's):
> Complete DSA sheet for placements 🚀 450 questions in 15 topics: arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks and queues, trees, graphs, dynamic programming. Start with arrays (40 questions), then move topic by topic. Do 5 questions a day and you finish in 3 months. Free sheet link in bio. #dsa #placements #coding

Note to type: **DSA prep ke liye, placement se pehle**

Tested against the live reading (same model and code): caption judged *informative*, type *tutorial_howto*, "what this is": *A placement preparation strategy and roadmap using a 450-question DSA sheet.* Key points (5): *450 questions across 15 topics*; *Start with arrays (40 questions)*; *Move topic by topic doing 5 questions daily*; *Schedule finishes preparation in 3 months*; *Free sheet link available in bio*. Ignored: *hashtags and emojis*.

**Dance Reel caption** (hashtag-heavy on purpose):
> Weekend vibes 💃🔥 #dance #reels #trending #dancechallenge #explore #viral @friend1 @friend2

Note to type: **red shirt**

Tested: caption judged *noise*, no key points from it, ignored: *Hashtags, mentions, emojis, and generic social phrases*. The "red shirt" idea comes only from the person's own words, as intended. The AI does not claim to see a shirt, because a shared Reel arrives without the video.

## Honesty on screen and in the voice-over

- The Instagram connection runs in Meta's development mode (approved testers).
- A shared Reel arrives as a link and caption, so the reading comes from the caption and the person's words.
- *Try without Instagram* is a simulated share and is labelled so.
- Shopping links are a concept (*Coming next*), not live.
- Do not quote accuracy beyond "small test".

## Recording checklist (8 lines)

1. Open the live site in a private window; phone with Instagram on a stand, hand from the right; record 30-second clips.
2. Part 1, shot 0:25: open `docs/video/saved-grid.html` in a phone-width browser and screen-record it; keep the *Dramatisation* label.
3. Connect Instagram with the code before recording Part 2, or record it live at 1:00.
4. Share the DSA Reel with the caption and note above; then the dance Reel with *red shirt*.
5. Wait 30 to 75 s for each save (record the wait separately and trim it).
6. If Instagram is slow: use **Try without Instagram** in the connect sheet; the save is labelled *Simulated Instagram share*; say so in the voice-over.
7. Record the voice-over from the Hindi lines; add the English subtitles; keep the total at or under 2:55.
8. Upload to YouTube (unlisted or public) or Drive (anyone with the link); open the link in a private window and check it plays and is under 3:00.
