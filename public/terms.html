What this is

Neophi is a prototype built for the Startup Buildathon India 2026 and shown for evaluation. It is provided as it is, with no promise that it will keep running or that data saved in it will be kept.

Your content

What you save and write stays yours. Only save and send things you have the right to keep a copy of. Neophi stores your saves as described on the Privacy page.

What the AI says

AI descriptions and suggestions can be wrong. They are labelled, show their evidence or say they are not sure, and are not a substitute for checking the source. The AI never changes your own words.
