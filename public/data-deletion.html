Data deletion

Last updated 20 November 2026

How to remove one save, or everything, including what came from Instagram.

Delete one save

  1. Open the save in Neophi.
  2. Choose Delete.

The link, your notes, any stored image or video, the AI reading and the history for that save are deleted. This cannot be undone.

Delete everything

  1. Open Settings in Neophi.
  2. Optionally choose Export all (JSON) to keep a copy first.
  3. Choose Delete everything and confirm.

All your saves, categories, projects and stored media are deleted. Reels you sent by Instagram direct message are deleted too, and are marked so that a message arriving late cannot bring them back. Your Instagram connection is removed, unused connection codes are deleted, and messages still waiting to be processed are dropped.

Disconnect Instagram only

  1. Open Settings, then Instagram.
  2. Choose Disconnect Instagram and confirm.

New messages from your Instagram account are no longer saved. Saves already made stay until you delete them.

What happens afterwards

  • Deleted files may remain recoverable inside Google Cloud for up to 7 days before they are removed for good.
  • Instagram processing records are deleted automatically within 7 days of the message.
  • Technical request logs expire within 30 days.
  • Your anonymous sign-in ID remains, with nothing attached to it. Clearing this site's data in your browser signs you out.
  • The original message stays in your Instagram conversation. Delete it there with Instagram's own tools.

If you can't use the app

Your library is tied to the browser you signed in with. If you no longer have that browser, or you sent Reels by Instagram and want them removed, send a request using the contact on the Privacy page. Include your Instagram username and roughly when you sent the messages.
