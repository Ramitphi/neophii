Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Neophi is a prototype built for the Startup Buildathon India 2026. This page describes what the app does today.

What Neophi stores

  • The links you save, their captions, and the notes you write, exactly as you typed them.
  • Images or short clips you upload (up to 15 MB and 60 seconds), and video from Instagram Reels you send us by direct message.
  • Your choices for each save: why you saved it, its category, projects, reminders, and whether it is private.
  • The AI reading of each save, and a history of what was added or changed and by whom.
  • An anonymous sign-in ID created for your browser. We do not ask for your name, email address or password.

Where it is stored

Your library is stored in Google Cloud: saves in Firestore and files in a private Cloud Storage bucket, both in Google's Mumbai region. Each library is kept separate and can only be read by the browser that signed in to it.

AI analysis

Unless you mark a save as private, its media, caption and your note are sent to Google's Gemini models through Vertex AI to describe the save, suggest a category and power search. These requests use Google's global endpoint, so they may be processed outside India. AI suggestions are labelled, show the evidence they rely on or say they are not sure, and never change your own words. Saves marked private are never sent to an AI model.

Reels sent by Instagram direct message

This works only for Instagram accounts the Neophi team has approved as testers. When you send a Reel or post to the Neophi Instagram account, Meta delivers to our server:

  • your Instagram-scoped ID (a number, not your username), the message ID and the time;
  • the shared post's link, caption and type, and a link to its media;
  • any text you send with it or shortly after, kept exactly as sent.

Our server checks Meta's signature on every delivery and ignores anything else. It keeps a processing record of each delivery for up to 7 days, then deletes it automatically; the message content in that record is cleared as soon as it is processed. Media is downloaded only from Meta's own media servers and stored privately in your library.

Neophi does not read your Instagram account, your followers, your other messages, or anything you did not send us. It never asks for your Instagram password.

Who else receives your data

  • Meta carries the Instagram messages. Its own policies apply.
  • Google hosts the app and its data and runs the AI analysis.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking scripts.

How long we keep it

Your saves stay until you delete them. Deleted files may remain recoverable inside Google Cloud for up to 7 days before they are removed for good. Instagram processing records are deleted automatically within 7 days.

Your choices

  • Mark any save as private so it is never sent to AI.
  • Export your whole library from Settings.
  • Delete one save, or everything. See Data deletion.
