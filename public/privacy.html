Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

What Neophi keeps, why, where it lives, who else sees it, and how to remove it.

Neophi is a prototype built for the Startup Buildathon India 2026. This page describes what the app does today. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

Why Neophi keeps anything

Neophi exists to keep the things you save online together with your own reason for saving them, sort them into categories, help you find them again, and bring them back when they are useful. We use your data only for that. We do not sell it, show advertising, or build profiles of you.

What Neophi stores

  • The links you save, their captions, and the notes you write, exactly as you typed them.
  • Images or short clips you upload (up to 15 MB and 60 seconds), and video from Instagram Reels you send us by direct message.
  • Your choices for each save: why you saved it, its category, projects, reminders, and whether it is private.
  • The AI reading of each save, a numeric search index made from it, and a history of what was added or changed and by whom.
  • If you connect Instagram, the link between your Instagram-scoped ID and your library.
  • An anonymous sign-in ID created for your browser. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.

Where it is stored

In Google Cloud: saves in Firestore and files in a private Cloud Storage bucket, both in Google's Mumbai region. Inside the app, your library can only be opened from the browser that created it. The Neophi team can technically access the stored data as administrators of the Google Cloud project, and does so only to run and fix the service or to act on a deletion request.

What is sent to AI

Neophi uses Google's Gemini models through Vertex AI. These requests use Google's global endpoint, so they may be processed outside India. Gemini receives:

  • a save's media, caption and your note, to describe it and suggest a category;
  • the words you type into search, to understand them in English, Hindi and Hinglish;
  • the saves in a project, when you ask Neophi to draft that project's outcome.

Saves you mark private are never sent to an AI model, in any of these cases. AI suggestions are labelled, show the evidence they rely on or say they are not sure, and never change your own words.

Connecting Instagram and sending Reels by direct message

You can connect your Instagram account to your library once: Neophi shows you a one-time code (it works once, for 15 minutes), and you send that code as a direct message to the Neophi Instagram account. We then store a link between your Instagram-scoped ID (a number, not your username) and your anonymous library. Only a fingerprint of the code is stored, never the code itself. From then on, when you send a Reel or post to the Neophi Instagram account, Meta delivers to our server:

  • your Instagram-scoped ID, the message ID and the time;
  • the shared post's link, caption and type, and a link to its media when Meta provides one;
  • any text you send with it or shortly after, kept exactly as you typed it and shown first on the save.

Our server checks Meta's signature on every delivery and ignores anything else. It keeps a processing record of each delivery for up to 7 days, then deletes it automatically. The message content in that record is cleared as soon as it has been processed. Media is downloaded only from Meta's own media servers and stored privately in your library. When Meta sends only a page link and no video, the save keeps the caption and your exact words.

Messages from Instagram accounts that are not connected to a library are not stored. To stop, open Settings, then Instagram, and choose Disconnect Instagram; saves already made stay until you delete them. Neophi does not send replies in Instagram.

Neophi does not read your Instagram account, your followers, your other messages, or anything you did not send us. It never asks for your Instagram password.

Who else receives your data

  • Meta carries the Instagram messages. Its own policies apply.
  • Google hosts the app and its data, runs the anonymous sign-in (Firebase Authentication), runs the AI analysis, and serves the web fonts Neophi uses.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

How long we keep it

  • Your saves stay until you delete them.
  • Deleted files may remain recoverable inside Google Cloud for up to 7 days before they are removed for good.
  • Instagram processing records are deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Google Cloud keeps technical request logs (such as IP address, browser and time) for up to 30 days to run and secure the service.

Your choices

  • Mark any save as private so it is never sent to AI.
  • Export your whole library from Settings.
  • Correct your notes and choices at any time.
  • Disconnect Instagram from Settings at any time.
  • Delete one save, or everything. See Data deletion.
